## Locations

### LOC-1: "Die Autobahn" (Szene 1)

```
A six-lane German Autobahn (three lanes each direction, divided by
a concrete center barrier) during blue hour at dusk. Heavy mixed
traffic: white and gray semi-trailers with curtainside and
refrigerated bodies, box trucks, sedans, SUVs. Wet dark asphalt
reflecting red tail lights and white headlights. Silver metal
guardrails on both sides. Green grass embankments sloping away
from the road. Overhead green Autobahn signs with white text in
the distance. Light mist in the air. The highway curves gently
to the right.
```

### LOC-2: "Die Leitstelle / Operations" (Szene 2.1)

```
A busy IoT operations center, approximately 5 meters wide and 4
meters deep. A large L-shaped desk dominates the room, with three
widescreen monitors (27-inch each) arranged in a slight curve. The
center monitor shows a dark-themed European map with hundreds of
small orange glowing dots (tracked assets) and faint route lines.
The left monitor shows a list view with status rows and colored
indicators. The right monitor shows a detail view with a single
asset route, temperature graph, and ETA countdown. A desk phone,
a coffee mug, a stack of printed route sheets, and a wireless
headset charger sit on the desk. One wall has a large printed
European map with handwritten sticky notes. Overhead fluorescent
office lighting, slightly warm. A glass wall on one side with a
view into a larger open-plan office. The room feels operational —
lived in, productive, under control.
```

### LOC-3: "Das Datacenter" (Szene 2.2)

```
A Tier-3 data center server room, approximately 15 meters deep.
Access through a heavy glass security door with a badge reader
mounted on the right wall. Inside: two rows of modern black server
racks (approximately 10 racks per row) in a hot-aisle/cold-aisle
configuration. Each rack has blue and orange LED status indicators
in perfect vertical rows. White raised flooring with perforated
tiles. Bright cool-white overhead fluorescent lighting. A
wall-mounted 24-inch monitor on the right wall near the entrance
shows a dark security dashboard with green checkmark icons and lock
symbols. Temperature displays and fire suppression system visible
on the far wall. Cable trays running along the ceiling. The room
is spotless, sterile, perfectly organized. Cool air visible.
```

### LOC-4: "Das Engineering-Büro" (Szene 2.3, 3.x)

```
A bright, modern open-plan office in a German tech company,

with trees and low buildings. Inside: light oak hardwood flooring.
A large wall-mounted whiteboard (approximately 2 meters wide,
```
