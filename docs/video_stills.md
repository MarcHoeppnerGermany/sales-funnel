# First Frames / Stills: "Die Plattform hinter Europas größter Trailer-Flotte"

## Verwendung

Diese Stills dienen als:
- **First Frames** für Higgsfield Video-Generierung (Konsistenz-Anker)
- **Standalone-Bilder** für LinkedIn-Posts, Pitch Decks, Website
- **Thumbnail** für das fertige Video

Jeder Still entspricht exakt einer der 3 Video-Szenen.

## Still 1 — "Die Straße" (First Frame für Video-Szene 1)

```
Aerial drone photograph of a German Autobahn at dusk, blue hour
lighting. Five modern white semi-trailers spread across a six-lane
highway, each in a different lane and at varying distances from
each other — some overtaking, some further ahead, one just entering
the frame. Realistic highway traffic pattern. Each trailer has a
small glowing orange rectangular HUD panel floating directly above
its roof — similar to a car head-up display reflection, minimal
and flat, showing a tiny GPS pin icon with a dotted route line,
a small thermometer icon next to a horizontal bar gauge, and a
circular tire pressure gauge. Between each trailer roof and the
HUD panel, thin orange concentric radio wave arcs pulse upward,
like a cell tower signal icon. The HUD panels and radio arcs are
subtle and translucent, integrated into the scene like augmented
reality — not sci-fi, more like a Bloomberg Terminal overlay on
reality. Wet asphalt reflecting orange and red tail lights.
Shot from 45-degree overhead drone angle.
[Style Anchor]
```

**Einsatz:** LinkedIn Hero Image, Video Thumbnail, Website Banner, Pitch Deck Cover

## Still 2 — "Die Plattform" (First Frame für Video-Szene 2)

```
Wide shot of a modern control room. In the foreground, the
silhouette of an engineer standing with arms crossed, back to
camera. He faces a large curved wall-mounted display showing a
dark-themed European map with hundreds of small orange glowing dots
representing tracked trailers, connected by faint route lines.
Subtle data visualizations — circular gauges, small spark lines —
at the edges of the screen. On the right side of the room, a heavy
glass security door is slightly open, revealing a pristine server
room behind it with rows of modern server racks, blue and orange
LED status lights glowing in perfect rows. Ambient blue-white room
lighting with warm orange reflections from the display. The image
conveys total oversight and absolute security.
[Style Anchor]
```

**Einsatz:** Key Visual — LinkedIn, Website, Pitch Deck, DOCX Deckblatt, TISAX/Security Slide

## Konsistenz-Checkliste

- [ ] **Alle 3 Stills generiert**
- [ ] **Orange (#E67E22) ist einzige Akzentfarbe** in jedem Bild
- [ ] **Keine frontalen Gesichter** — Silhouette, Seite, Over-Shoulder
- [ ] **Kein lesbarer Code/Text auf Bildschirmen**
- [ ] **Gleiche Farbtemperatur** — teal/blau Schatten, warme Orange-Highlights
- [ ] **Konsistenter Film-Look** — Grain, Shallow DOF, Anamorphic
- [ ] **Still 2 als Referenz-Bild** für Stil-Vergleich bei den anderen
