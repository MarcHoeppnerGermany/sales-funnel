### LOC-1: "Die Autobahn" (Szene 1)

```

```

office lighting, slightly warm. A glass wall on one side with a
view into a larger open-plan office. The room feels operational —
lived in, productive, under control.
```

```

tiles. Bright cool-white overhead fluorescent lighting. A
wall-mounted 24-inch monitor on the right wall near the entrance
shows a dark security dashboard with green checkmark icons and lock
symbols. Temperature displays and fire suppression system visible
