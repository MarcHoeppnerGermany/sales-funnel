# First Frames / Stills: "Die Plattform hinter Europas größter Trailer-Flotte"

## Verwendung

- **First Frames** für Video-Generierung (Konsistenz-Anker)
- **Standalone-Bilder** für LinkedIn-Posts, Pitch Decks, Website
- **Thumbnail** für das fertige Video

Jeder Still entspricht exakt einem der 6 Shots im Video-Script.
Style Anchor ist direkt in jedem Prompt eingebaut — einfach den ganzen Block kopieren.
Alle Prompts für **SeaDance 2.0** optimiert.

**2 Szenen à 3 Shots = 6 Shots à ~5s = ~30s Gesamtlänge**

## Szene 1 — "Die Straße"

**3 Perspektiven:** Weit oben → Seitlich mitfahrend → Umflug, DCU-Detail.

### Still 1.1 — Totale Autobahn, Drohne nähert sich

> Dynamischer Overhead-Shot: Kamera ist weit weg und bewegt sich auf einen Autobahnabschnitt zu.

```
Wide aerial drone photograph shot from high above, looking down at
a steep 60-degree angle onto a busy German Autobahn during blue
hour at dusk. Six-lane highway (three lanes each direction, divided
by concrete center barrier) with heavy mixed traffic: white and
gray semi-trailers with curtainside and refrigerated bodies, box
trucks, sedans, SUVs in both directions. Wet dark asphalt reflecting
hundreds of red tail lights and white headlights. Silver metal
guardrails on both sides. Green grass embankments. Overhead green
Autobahn signs in the distance. Light mist in the air. The highway
curves gently to the right. No special effects, no overlays, no
glowing elements. Pure photorealistic highway scene. The camera
angle suggests the drone is descending toward the highway. Cinematic
corporate photography, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field,
color graded with teal shadows and warm orange highlights (#E67E22),
natural lighting, shot on ARRI Alexa, muted color palette with
selective orange accents, 16:9 aspect ratio, photorealistic, high
detail, 8K resolution
```

The rest of the trailer is clean and photorealistic. Highway traffic
is blurred in the background behind the trailer. Wet asphalt, blue
hour lighting, light mist. Cinematic corporate photography,
