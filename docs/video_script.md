# Video Script: "Die Plattform hinter Europas größter Trailer-Flotte"

## Format & Spezifikationen

- **Länge:** ~45 Sekunden (3 Szenen à max. 15s)
- **Struktur:** 3 Szenen × 3 Shots = 9 Shots à ~5s
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (LinkedIn / Website)
- **Untertitel:** Immer eingeblendet (80% schauen ohne Ton)
- **Musik:** Ambient Tech-Track, seriös, kein Beat-Drop
- **Text-Overlays in Post-Production** (CapCut / DaVinci Resolve)
- **Schnitt in Post** — Shots einzeln generieren, zusammensetzen
- **Style Anchor ist direkt in jedem Prompt eingebaut**
- **Alle Prompts für SeaDance 2.0 optimiert**

Charakter- und Location-Beschreibungen: siehe `video_stills.md`

**Story:** Asset auf der Straße → DCU sendet Daten → Operations Manager empfängt und steuert → Datacenter verarbeitet → Engineering-Team hat alles gebaut (TISAX) → Betrieb trifft Entwicklung → Einladung zum Kontakt

**Regisseur-Referenz (B2B Sales Director):**
- Szene 1: Murro (Tracking, Teal-Orange) + Fincher (DCU-Detail)
- Szene 2: Morris (Disponent, statisch, echt) + Murro (Datacenter, Team)
- Szene 3: Morris + Fincher Hybrid ("Authoritative Precision")

**Kommunikationsziel:** 60% Relevanz ("Die verstehen mein Problem") + 40% Beweis ("Die können liefern")
**Zielgruppe:** CTO/CIO in Mittelstand und Konzern — branchenübergreifend IoT

## Szene 1 — "Die Straße" (0-15s)

**3 Perspektiven:** Weit oben → Seitlich mitfahrend → Umflug, DCU-Detail.

### Shot 1.1 — Totale Autobahn, Drohne nähert sich (0-5s)

> SeaDance Input: Still 1.1 als First Frame. Drohne fliegt von weit oben näher an die Autobahn.

```
Aerial drone shot starting from very high above a busy German
Autobahn at dusk during blue hour. Camera at a steep 60-degree
downward angle onto the six-lane highway — three lanes each
direction divided by a concrete center barrier. Heavy mixed traffic:
white curtainside semi-trailers, refrigerated trailers with
roof-mounted cooling units, box trucks, dark sedans, silver SUVs.
Wet dark asphalt reflects hundreds of red tail lights streaming away
and white headlights approaching, creating long parallel streaks on
the glistening road — red receding, white advancing, the entire
highway alive with movement. Silver metal guardrails line both
sides, their reflective surfaces catching flashes of passing
headlights. Green grass embankments slope away into gathering
darkness, the grass still holding the last green of daylight.
Overhead green Autobahn signs with white text and route numbers in
the distance, slightly obscured by light mist hanging in the cool
evening air. A thin layer of ground fog drifts above the
embankments, diffusing the light from below. The highway curves
gently right, traffic flowing like a river of lights around the
bend. The camera smoothly and steadily descends toward a flatter
perspective approximately 30 meters above the road — vehicles
growing larger, individual trailers becoming distinguishable,
rooftops reflecting the last ambient dusk light. No overlays, no
HUD elements, no glowing effects — purely realistic. The descent
is slow and controlled, creating a sense of approaching something
important. Color temperature shifts subtly from cool blue at height
to warmer tones as the camera nears the highway. Cinematic corporate film, anamorphic
lens with subtle horizontal flare, shallow depth of field, color
graded with deep teal shadows and warm orange highlights (#E67E22),
available dusk lighting only, shot on ARRI Alexa 35, 24fps natural
film grain, muted palette with selective orange accents from tail
lights, 16:9 aspect ratio
```

### Shot 1.3 — Umflug über Trailer, DCU sendet Daten (10-15s)

> SeaDance Input: Still 1.3 als First Frame. Kamera ist auf die andere Seite geflogen, DCU hinter Stirnwand/Kingpin sichtbar.

```
Aerial drone shot from the opposite side of a single white
curtainside semi-trailer driving on a German Autobahn at dusk, blue
hour. Camera on the left (oncoming traffic) side, slightly above
roof height, looking across at the trailer's front section. The
flat white front wall (Stirnwand) is clearly visible, surface
slightly weathered. Below it, near the kingpin coupling where
trailer connects to tractor, a small black rectangular electronic
box is mounted — the digital control unit (DCU), approximately
20×15cm, bolted to a metal bracket with a short antenna stub on
top. The DCU is highlighted with a subtle warm orange (#E67E22)
glow outline that pulses gently. From the DCU, thin orange
concentric radio wave arcs radiate outward and upward — three to
four arcs expanding like a cell tower signal icon, each fading as
it grows. The arcs pulse rhythmically, a visual heartbeat of data
transmission. A faint orange dotted data stream line extends from
the outermost arc upward at 30 degrees into the darkening sky —
telemetry traveling to the cloud. The trailer drives at highway
speed, wheels motion-blurred. Background traffic compressed into
streaked bokeh — white and red light trails flattened by telephoto.
Tractor cab partially visible but out of focus. Camera holds steady,
locked off, isolating the DCU as the technical centerpiece. Wet
asphalt reflects faint light. Light mist diffuses ambient light.
The mood is technical, precise — this small box is the origin of
all the data. Cinematic corporate film, anamorphic lens 85-135mm
telephoto, extreme shallow depth of field isolating DCU and front
wall, color graded with deep teal shadows and concentrated orange
(#E67E22) from DCU, available dusk lighting, shot on ARRI Alexa 35,
24fps natural film grain, muted palette with orange DCU energy as
sole accent, 16:9 aspect ratio
```
