# Video Script: "Die Plattform hinter Europas größter Trailer-Flotte"

## Format & Spezifikationen

- **Länge:** 45-60 Sekunden (3 Szenen à 15-20s)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (LinkedIn / Website)
- **Untertitel:** Immer eingeblendet (80% schauen ohne Ton)
- **Musik:** Ambient Tech-Track, seriös, kein Beat-Drop
- **Text-Overlays in Post-Production** (CapCut / DaVinci Resolve)
- **Keine Voiceover nötig** — Text-Overlays wirken moderner
- **3 Szenen** — jede Szene einzeln in Higgsfield generieren, in Post zusammenschneiden

---

## Style Anchor

> Wird an **jeden** Higgsfield-Prompt angehängt.

```
Cinematic corporate film, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field,
color graded with teal shadows and warm orange highlights (#E67E22),
natural lighting, shot on ARRI Alexa, 24fps film grain,
muted color palette with selective orange accents, 16:9 aspect ratio
```

## Szene 1 — "Die Straße" (0-20s)

Hook + Problem + Lösung in einem Bild: Vernetzte Trailer auf der Autobahn.

**Video Prompt:**
```
Aerial drone shot slowly tracking alongside a German Autobahn at
dusk, blue hour lighting. Five modern white semi-trailers spread
across a six-lane highway, each in a different lane and at varying
distances from each other — some overtaking, some further ahead,
one just entering the frame. Realistic highway traffic pattern.
Each trailer has a small glowing orange rectangular HUD panel
floating directly above its roof — similar to a car head-up display
reflection, minimal and flat, showing a tiny GPS pin icon with a
dotted route line, a small thermometer icon next to a horizontal
bar gauge, and a circular tire pressure gauge. Between each trailer
roof and the HUD panel, thin orange concentric radio wave arcs
pulse upward, like a cell tower signal icon. The HUD panels and
radio arcs are subtle and translucent, integrated into the scene
like augmented reality — not sci-fi, more like a Bloomberg Terminal
overlay on reality. Wet asphalt reflecting orange and red tail
lights. Smooth lateral drone tracking movement.
[Style Anchor]
```

**Text-Overlays (Post):**
> *"Was passiert, wenn jeder Trailer ein IoT-Gerät wird?"* (0-5s)
>
> *"Zehntausende Einheiten. 20+ Länder. Echtzeit."* (5-12s)
>
> *"Architektur. Code. Betrieb. Alles aus einer Hand."* (12-20s)

## Szene 2 — "Die Plattform" (20-40s)

Von der Straße in die Leitstelle: Kontrolle, Sicherheit, Zertifizierung.

**Video Prompt:**
```
Camera slowly dollies forward into a modern control room. In the
foreground, the silhouette of an engineer standing with arms crossed,
back to camera. He faces a large curved wall-mounted display showing
a dark-themed European map with hundreds of small orange glowing
dots representing tracked trailers, connected by faint route lines.
Subtle data visualizations — circular gauges, small spark lines —
animate softly at the edges of the screen. The camera continues
past the engineer and pushes toward a heavy glass security door on
the right side of the room, which opens to reveal a pristine server
room behind it. Rows of modern server racks with blue and orange
LED status lights glowing in perfect rows. Everything is spotless,
organized, controlled. The atmosphere conveys total oversight and
absolute security. Smooth, continuous dolly movement, no cuts.
[Style Anchor]
```

>
> *"TISAX Stufe 3 — höchste Sicherheitsstufe"* (26-33s)

## Szene 3 — "Die Ingenieure" (40-60s)

Das Team dahinter + CTA: Wer wir sind, was wir können, Kontakt.

**Video Prompt:**
```
Medium shot in a bright, modern German office with large
floor-to-ceiling windows and natural daylight. A small team of
three software engineers stands around a large whiteboard covered
with architecture diagrams drawn in orange and blue markers. One
person is pointing at a diagram node, the other two are engaged in
discussion. Shot from a slight angle, not frontal — capturing the
team dynamic from the side. The camera slowly dollies sideways and
past the team, coming to rest on a close-up of a clean white desk
in the foreground. A hand places a business card on the desk
surface — minimalist design with an orange accent bar and dark
charcoal text. The background softens into a warm bright blur.
The final frame is the card on the desk, sharp and elegant.
Smooth, continuous movement.
[Style Anchor]
```
