# Video Script: "Die Plattform hinter Europas größter Trailer-Flotte"

## Format & Spezifikationen

- **Länge:** ~45 Sekunden (3 Szenen à max. 15s)
- **Struktur:** 3 Szenen × 3 Shots = 9 Shots à ~5s
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (LinkedIn / Website)
- **Untertitel:** Immer eingeblendet (80% schauen ohne Ton)
- **Musik:** Ambient Tech-Track, seriös, kein Beat-Drop
- **Text-Overlays in Post-Production** (CapCut / DaVinci Resolve)
- **Schnitt in Post** — Shots einzeln generieren, zusammensetzen

---

## Szene 1 — "Die Straße" (0-15s)

### Shot 1.1 — Totale Autobahn (0-5s)

```
Wide aerial drone shot of a German Autobahn at dusk, blue hour
lighting. Five modern white semi-trailers spread across a six-lane
highway, each in a different lane and at varying distances — some
overtaking, some further ahead. Realistic highway traffic pattern.
Wet asphalt reflecting red tail lights. No overlays, no effects.
Camera slowly drifts laterally over the highway.
[Style Anchor]
```

### Shot 1.2 — HUD-Overlays erscheinen (5-10s)

```
Same aerial perspective over the Autobahn at dusk. Now each trailer
has a small glowing orange rectangular HUD panel floating above its
roof — similar to a car head-up display, minimal and flat, showing
a tiny GPS pin icon with a dotted route line, a small thermometer
icon next to a horizontal bar gauge, and a circular tire pressure
gauge. The panels are translucent and subtle, like augmented reality.
Camera continues lateral tracking, steady drone movement.
[Style Anchor]
```

### Shot 1.3 — Funkverkehr / Vernetzung (10-15s)

```
Closer aerial shot focusing on two trailers on the highway at dusk.
Orange HUD panels glow above each roof. Between each trailer and
its HUD panel, thin orange concentric radio wave arcs pulse upward
like a cell tower signal icon. A faint orange dotted line connects
the two trailers' HUD panels to each other, suggesting a data
network. The arcs and connection lines are translucent, integrated
into the scene like a Bloomberg Terminal overlay on reality. Wet
road, blue hour light, orange reflections.
[Style Anchor]
```

## Szene 2 — "Die Plattform" (15-30s)

### Shot 2.1 — Control Room Totale (15-20s)

```
Wide shot of a modern, dimly lit control room. A large curved
wall-mounted display dominates the back wall, showing a dark-themed
European map with hundreds of small orange glowing dots representing
tracked trailers, connected by faint route lines. Subtle circular
gauges and spark lines at the edges of the screen. The room is
empty, the display glows. Ambient blue-white lighting with warm
orange reflections from the screen. Camera slowly dollies forward
toward the display.
[Style Anchor]
```

### Shot 2.2 — Ingenieur vor der Karte (20-25s)

```
Over-the-shoulder shot from behind a single engineer standing in
the control room, arms crossed, silhouetted against the large
curved display. The European map with hundreds of pulsing orange
dots fills the screen in front of him. His silhouette is dark and
sharp against the glowing map. Camera is static, slight handheld
movement. The atmosphere is calm, controlled, powerful.
[Style Anchor]
```

### Shot 2.3 — Server Room / Sicherheit (25-30s)

```
Camera pushes slowly through a heavy glass security door into a
pristine server room. Rows of modern server racks stretch into the
distance, blue and orange LED status lights glowing in perfect rows.
Everything is spotless, organized, controlled. The light is cool
and clean. A wall-mounted screen shows a security dashboard with
green checkmark icons and lock symbols. The atmosphere conveys
absolute trust, reliability, and restricted access. Smooth slow
dolly forward.
[Style Anchor]
```

## Szene 3 — "Die Ingenieure" (30-45s)

### Shot 3.1 — Team am Whiteboard (30-35s)

```
Medium shot in a bright, modern German office with large
floor-to-ceiling windows and natural daylight streaming in. Three
software engineers stand around a large whiteboard covered with
architecture diagrams drawn in orange and blue markers. One person
points at a diagram node, the others listen and nod. Shot from a
slight angle, capturing the team dynamic from the side — no direct
frontal faces. Professional but relaxed, smart casual clothing.
Camera is static with subtle handheld sway.
[Style Anchor]
```

### Shot 3.2 — Detail Whiteboard (35-40s)

```
Close-up of a whiteboard in a bright office. Architecture diagrams
drawn in orange and blue markers — boxes connected by arrows,
labeled with small icons, not readable text. A hand holding an
orange marker draws a new connecting line between two nodes. Shallow
depth of field, the background is a soft blur of the bright office
and windows. Warm natural light. Camera is static, focused on the
hand and the diagram.
[Style Anchor]
```

### Shot 3.3 — Visitenkarte / CTA (40-45s)

```
Extreme close-up of a hand placing a business card on a clean white
desk surface. The card shows a minimalist design with an orange
accent bar on the left, dark charcoal text, and the URL
"neogeo.com". Shallow depth of field, the desk surface has a subtle
texture. The background is a soft warm blur of a bright office with
large windows. One finger still touching the card. Camera slowly
racks focus from the card to the bright window behind. Minimal,
premium, elegant.
[Style Anchor]
```
