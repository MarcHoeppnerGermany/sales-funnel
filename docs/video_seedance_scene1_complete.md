# Seedance 2.0 — Szene 1 "Die Straße" (Komplett-Prompt)

## Verwendung

Ein einziger 15-Sekunden-Prompt für die komplette Szene 1 (3 Shots).
Alle Cuts, Kamerabewegungen und Übergänge sind im Prompt enthalten.

Still 1.1 als First Frame hochladen, dann den EN- oder ZH-Prompt kopieren.

---

## EN Prompt

```
Style & Mood: Blue hour dusk, desaturated teal-and-steel palette. Selective warm orange (#E67E22) confined to tail lights, HUD panels, and DCU signal arcs — everything else cool and muted. Anamorphic 2.39:1 framing, 24fps film grain, shallow depth of field on telephoto passes. Wet-surface reflections carry most of the color. Corporate documentary gravity, no music-video flash. Narrative Summary: A drone descends onto a busy German highway at dusk, reveals augmented-reality telemetry hovering above cargo trailers, then isolates a single trailer's underside control unit transmitting data skyward. Dynamic Description: Aerial drone crane-descent from extreme wide — the six-lane Autobahn fills the lower frame, traffic streaming in both directions. The camera tilts from steep bird's-eye, gradually flattening as altitude drops. White curtainside semi-trailers, refrigerated boxes, sedans, and SUVs crawl below, red tail lights bleeding left-to-right in the near lanes, white headlights flowing right-to-left opposite. The highway bends gently right. Guardrail silver and embankment green sharpen as the camera closes distance. No overlays, no graphics — raw highway. Hard cut to stabilized aerial tracking, 45-degree side angle above and behind the traffic flow. The camera matches highway speed. Three white curtainside trailers stagger across the right lanes — near, mid, far. Above each roof, a small glowing orange rectangular HUD panel hovers, flat and translucent like a head-up display reflection. Each panel holds a GPS pin icon trailing a dotted route line and a thermometer icon beside a horizontal bar gauge. The panels drift locked to their trailers, edges softly lit. Cars and box trucks stream past in the left lanes. Asphalt streaks with motion blur beneath. Hard cut to medium close-up, telephoto compression — the camera now sits on the opposite side of a single white curtainside trailer, oncoming-traffic side, at roof height. The trailer drives left-to-right. Its front wall is fully visible. Mounted on the underside directly behind the front wall near the kingpin coupling: a small black rectangular unit, the DCU. A subtle orange glow traces its outline. Thin concentric radio-wave arcs radiate from the DCU outward and upward, pulsing at rhythmic intervals — a cell-tower signal icon made physical. A faint orange data-stream line extends from the arcs into the darkening sky above. Background traffic compresses into horizontal streaked bokeh. The camera holds locked alongside, steady on the DCU as arcs pulse. Static Description: Six-lane divided German Autobahn, concrete center barrier, silver guardrails, mown green embankments. Overhead green signs with white text receding. Light ground-level mist. Wet dark asphalt. White curtainside semi-trailers, refrigerated trailers, box trucks, mixed passenger vehicles. Orange HUD panels: flat, translucent, AR-style — GPS pin with dotted route, thermometer with bar gauge. DCU: small black rectangular box, underside-mounted behind trailer front wall, near kingpin. Orange signal arcs: concentric, translucent, pulsing upward. Faint orange data line ascending into sky.
```

## ZH Prompt

```
风格与氛围：蓝色时刻黄昏，低饱和青钢色调。选择性暖橙（#E67E22）仅限于尾灯、HUD面板与DCU信号弧——其余一切冷调克制。变形宽银幕2.39:1构图，24帧胶片颗粒，长焦段浅景深。湿润路面反射承载主要色彩。企业纪录片质感。动态描述：航拍摇臂从极远景下降——六车道高速公路填满画面下部，车流双向涌动。镜头从陡峭鸟瞰逐渐压平，高度持续下降。白色侧帘半挂车、冷藏厢车、轿车、SUV在下方缓行，近车道红色尾灯从左向右拖曳，对向白色前灯从右向左流动。公路向右缓弯。护栏银光与路堤草绿随距离缩短逐渐锐化。无叠加图形，纯粹公路实景。硬切至稳定航拍跟拍，45度侧上方略偏后方跟随车流。摄影机匹配公路速度。三辆白色侧帘拖车在右侧车道交错排列——近景、中景、远景。每辆车顶正上方悬浮小型发光橙色矩形HUD面板，扁平半透明如抬头显示反射。每个面板包含GPS定位图标配虚线路径及温度计图标旁水平条形仪表。面板锁定拖车漂移，边缘柔和发光。轿车与厢式货车在左侧车道流过。沥青路面运动模糊拉丝。硬切至中近景长焦压缩——摄影机移至单辆白色侧帘拖车对面，对向车道侧，车顶高度。拖车从左向右行驶。前壁完全可见。底部紧贴前壁后方近主销处安装小型黑色矩形单元——DCU。微妙橙色光晕勾勒轮廓。纤细同心射频波弧从DCU向外向上辐射，节奏性脉冲跳动如实体化基站信号图标。淡橙数据流线从波弧延伸至渐暗上空。背景车流压缩为水平拉丝散景。摄影机锁定DCU侧方跟随，波弧持续脉冲。静态描述：六车道德国高速公路，混凝土中央隔离带，银色护栏，绿色草坡路堤。远处绿底白字路牌。低层薄雾。深色湿润沥青。白色侧帘半挂车、冷藏拖车、厢式货车、混合客车。橙色HUD面板：扁平半透明AR风格。DCU：黑色矩形盒体，前壁后方底部安装近主销处。橙色信号弧同心半透明向上脉冲。淡橙数据线升入天空。
```
